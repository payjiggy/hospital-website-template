Services

Comprehensive Specialist Medical Services

General Medicine

Our General Medicine unit provides expert diagnosis and treatment for a wide range of adult health conditions. Our experienced physicians focus on preventive care, accurate diagnosis, and personalized treatment plans for every patient.

Surgery & Laparoscopic Procedures

Our modern operating theatres support both minor and major surgeries, including advanced laparoscopic procedures. With skilled surgeons and advanced monitoring equipment, we ensure safe and effective surgical care.

Outpatient Consultation

Our outpatient services allow patients to consult specialist doctors across multiple medical fields including Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Internal Medicine, Dermatology, and Orthopedics.

Emergency & Medical Evacuation

Sterling Specialist Hospital provides 24-hour emergency medical care and evacuation services, ensuring rapid response and life-saving treatment for critical health situations.

Pharmacy Services

Our well-stocked pharmacy is managed by professional pharmacists and technicians who provide quality medications and personalized pharmaceutical care for all patients.

Laboratory & Diagnostic Testing

Our laboratory is equipped with advanced diagnostic technology for routine tests, cardiac markers, hormone assays, tumor markers, and blood bank services to support accurate medical diagnosis and treatment.
